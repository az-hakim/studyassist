## Voice

Calm, specific and encouraging. Never condescending. Short sentences, concrete verbs,
no exclamation marks in product copy. The tutor explains *how* to study as well as *what*
to study.

## Visual identity

- Accent: indigo → violet gradient (`#6366f1` → `#8b5cf6`).
- Surfaces: near-white `#f7f7fb` with white cards; dark mode `#0b0b12` with `#14141f` cards.
- Typography: Inter with a system-ui fallback, no network font requests.
- Icons: [lucide](https://lucide.dev), 1.75 px stroke.
- Radius: 12 px for controls, 16–20 px for cards, 999 px for pills.
- Illustration: none borrowed. Decorative gradients and original SVG icons only.
